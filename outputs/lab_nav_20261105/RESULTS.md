A trace shows the vehicle driving to 0.679 m from the goal and then commanding zero for the rest of the episode -- stopping beat risking the last metre, because contact costs -5.0 and idling costs -0.01 per step.
- Charging 0.03 per metre of remaining distance every step removes that trap: the same budget and goal pool goes from 0/8 to 4/8 with a mean final distance of 0.613 m.  The fixed goals and the held-out goals stay at zero, so finishing a practised goal and generalising are separate problems and only the first was fixed.
- Returns are not comparable across the reward variants: the distance charge lowers the return of a successful episode from about +5.9 to +2.8, so arrival counts must be compared, not mean return.
